Architecture Lab

Office Campus Setup

Configure the physical placement and logical settings for a dual-building corporate office consisting of Building A and Building B (separated by 50 feet with no physical cabling link).

Architectural Requirements

  • All incoming Internet traffic must pass through inspection before entering internal subnets.
  • Desktop workstations in Building A must not see unicast frames intended for other devices (switched segmentation). Port Security & VLAN 10 must be enforced.
  • Building B relies on an encrypted 2.4 GHz wireless bridge connection utilizing standard non-overlapping channels (Channels 1, 6, 11).
  • All wireless connectivity must enforce WPA2 - Enterprise authentication.
Instructions: Drag devices to the unlabeled drop zones. Click the gear icon (⚙️) on placed items to configure options.
